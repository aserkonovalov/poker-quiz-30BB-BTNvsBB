30bb BTN vs BB
Flop C-Bet Strategy

Simplified GTO — Villain checks, you decide

Situation: You open BTN 2bb, BB calls. Flop comes. BB checks.
SPR: ~4.5 (shallow)
Sizings: Small = 25% pot (~1/4) | Big = 62% pot (~2/3)
Position: You are IP (last to act)
Default: Bet 80% small
When in doubt, bet small. You can't go far wrong.
5 Rules by High Card
Rule 1: Ace-High Board
BET 100% — Mix sizing
Bet every time. Use big on dry boards, small on connected.
Examples:
A♠7♥2♣ (dry) → Bet big (62%)
A♥K♠9♣ (Broadway) → Bet small (25%)
A♥J♦T♣ (connected) → Bet small (25%)
A♦5♣3♥ (straight-possible) → Bet small (25%)
Rule 2: King or Queen-High Board
BET 100% SMALL (25% pot)
Always bet. Always small. Pure and simple.
Examples:
K♥8♦2♣ → Bet small
K♠Q♥4♦ → Bet small
Q♥7♦3♣ → Bet small
K♦6♥3♣ → Bet small
Rule 3: Jack or Ten-High Board
BET 75% small • CHECK 25%
Bet most of the time (small), but mix in some checks. Check more on connected textures.
Examples:
J♥7♦2♣ (dry) → Bet small (most of the time)
T♠8♥3♣ (slightly connected) → Bet small, sometimes check
J♦T♥8♣ (very connected) → Check more often
T♥9♦7♣ (straight board) → Check ~40%
Rule 4: Low Connected Board (7-high or less, connected)
CHECK 40% • Bet 60% BIG (62% pot)
This is your WORST texture. Check often. When you do bet, go big (polarized).
Examples:
6♥5♦4♣ (straight) → Check 40%, bet big 60%
7♠6♦5♣ (straight) → Check 40%, bet big 60%
5♥4♦3♣ (straight) → Check 40%, bet big 60%
7♥5♠4♦ (OESD) → Check ~35%, bet big
Rule 5: Low Unconnected Board (9-high or less, disconnected)
BET 75% • Mix sizing
Still bet most of the time, but mix your sizing between small and big.
Examples:
9♥6♦3♣ (rainbow dry) → Bet big more often
8♠5♥2♣ (disconnected) → Bet, mix sizing
9♦5♣2♥ (dry low) → Bet, lean big
8♥4♦2♣ (very dry) → Bet big
Modifiers (adjust from base rule)
🎲
Paired board (e.g., K♥K♦5♣, 9♠9♦3♣)
Check a bit more (+10-20% check). When you bet → small.
🎰
Trips board (e.g., 2♥2♦2♣, T♠T♦T♥)
Always bet. Use big sizing ~50% of the time.
🎨
Monotone board (e.g., A♠8♠5♠, K♥9♥7♥)
Still bet (don't slow down!), but shift to BIGGER sizing. Charge the draws.
🔗
Connected texture (e.g., J♥T♦9♣, 8♠7♥6♦)
Check more than default. When you do bet → prefer big.
Understanding Air (Why You Check)
What is "Air" in your range?
Air = hands that completely missed the board. No pair, no draw, no equity. When too much of your range is air, you must check more often — otherwise you're bluffing too much and villain can exploit you.
Where is your air HIGH? (check more)
• Low connected boards: 6♥5♦4♣, 7♠6♦5♣ — your AK, AQ, KJ, QJ, KT, JT all missed completely
• Low disconnected: 8♠4♥2♣ — only overpairs hit; your suited connectors and broadway are dead

Where is your air LOW? (bet freely)
• A-high boards: A♠7♥2♣ — your AA, AK, AQ, AJ, AT all connect. Most of your range has something.
• K/Q-high: K♥8♦2♣ — KK, AK, KQ, KJ, KT + overpairs all hit.
• Broadway: Q♥J♠T♣ — AK, KQ, KT, QJ, JT, AQ, 98 all connect.

This is exactly why: A/K/Q-high = bet 100% (low air). Low connected = check 40% (high air).
One-Line Summary
"Bet everything small on high boards.
Check more and size up on low connected boards."
That's 90% of the strategy.
