Key Concepts: Polar & Air (tap to expand)

POLAR RANGE = Your range has lots of very strong hands (sets, top pair good kicker, overpairs) AND lots of draws/bluffs (flush draws, gutshots, overcards). Very few medium-strength hands (weak top pair, second pair).

Example: You raise BTN, BB calls. Flop A♠7♥2♣. Your range: AA/AK/AQ (nuts) + 5♠4♠/T♠9♠ (bluffs). Few mediocre Ax. = Very polar.

Non-polar example: Flop K♠8♥5♣. Your range: KJ/KT/K9 (medium), 99/TT (medium). Lots of okay-but-not-great hands. = Not very polar.

AIR = Hands that completely missed the board. No pair, no draw, no real equity. Just overcards or random cards.

High air example: You raise BTN wide. Flop 7♥5♠4♣. Your range: AK, AQ, KJ, QJ, T9 — most of these missed completely. >15% of your range is pure air.

Low air example: Flop A♠K♥7♣. Your range: AA, AK, AQ, AJ, KQ, KJ + flush draws. Almost everything connects. Very little air.

How to estimate your air: Think about your full opening range on this flop. What % completely missed? If most of your suited connectors, broadway combos, and pocket pairs missed → high air. If the board is A-high or K-high and you opened from EP → low air (tight range connects).
