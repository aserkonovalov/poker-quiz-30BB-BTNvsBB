How to memorize these ranges

Mental hooks — the quiz data is the ground truth.

Open: big picture

  • 25BB = raise or fold (no jam). 15BB = raise mostly, jam a little (tightest open). 10BB = jam dominates.

Suited vs offsuit (#1 rule)

  • Suited goes much deeper than its offsuit twin. Unsure? Suited leans play, offsuit leans fold.

Aces & pairs

  • Every suited ace plays at every depth; low ones (A2s–A5s) love to jam short.
  • 25BB: pairs 33+ open. 15/10BB: small pairs jam, big pairs min-raise to trap.

15BB: after you min-raise and face a jam (call/fold)

  • You only reach this with hands you min-raised (grey = you’d have jammed or folded pre, so N/A).
  • Call with your value: big pairs (66+), strong aces (A7s+, AJo+/AQo), KQs+, broadway suited. Fold the speculative min-raises (suited gappers, weak offsuit broadways).
  • Call slightly wider vs the SB jam than vs the BB jam (e.g. KQo calls vs SB, folds vs BB) — the SB jams a bit wider, so you defend a touch more.
